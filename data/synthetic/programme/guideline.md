# Project Champion Programme Guideline (SYNTHETIC)

## 1. About this document

SYNTHETIC. This guideline was written by Team HFMD Aur Samose as a placeholder for the Project Champion programme guideline. Every number below is a plausible placeholder and must be validated with Hyderabad Runners Society before real use.

## 2. Programme purpose

Project Champion brings structured running sessions to government school children so that every child builds fitness, confidence and a habit of exercise.

## 3. Who the programme serves

Children in classes 6 and 7, aged roughly 11 to 13, in partner government schools in Hyderabad.

## 4. Roles

Each school has one HRS coach who runs sessions and one PE teacher who keeps attendance and runs fitness assessments. A programme coordinator oversees several schools.

## 5. Session frequency

Two sessions a week, on Tuesday and Friday, for six weeks per cycle: 12 planned sessions.

## 6. Session structure

Warm up (10 min), drills (15 min), main run or relay (15 min), cool down and stretching (5 min).

## 7. Attendance recording

The PE teacher marks each child P (present) or A (absent) on the weekly sheet for every session. A blank cell means the session was not recorded.

## 8. Minimum attendance

A child is expected to attend at least 60% of recorded sessions. Below 60% the child needs coaching attention.

## 9. Enough information to judge

A child's progress can only be judged when at least 6 of the 12 sessions have recorded attendance and the midline assessment has been done.

## 10. Coach session form

After every session the coach fills a short form naming children who stood out and any concerns. The form is not a register and does not replace attendance.

## 11. Fitness assessments

Three tests: 50m sprint, standing broad jump and 600m run. Baseline in week 1 and midline in week 6.

## 12. Fitness decline

If a child's 50m sprint or 600m run time at midline is more than 8% slower than baseline, the child needs coaching attention.

## 13. Expected improvement

Most children improve by 1 to 5% between baseline and midline. No improvement on its own is not a concern.

## 14. Repeated concerns

If the coach or teacher raises a concern about the same child two or more times in a cycle, the child needs coaching attention.

## 15. What counts as a concern

Fatigue that stops participation, pain or injury complaints, breathlessness, or repeated withdrawal from activities.

## 16. Injuries

Any injury complaint is recorded and the child rests until the coach clears them. Pain that repeats is escalated to the coordinator.

## 17. Conflicting records

If two records about a child disagree, for example the coach reports a child ran on a day the attendance sheet marks them absent, the coordinator checks both sources before acting.

## 18. WhatsApp group

Coaches, teachers and the coordinator use one WhatsApp group per school for updates. Messages are informal and do not replace the attendance sheet.

## 19. Names

Use the child's name as it appears on the school roster when possible.

## 20. Privacy

Do not share children's phone numbers, photos or addresses outside the programme.

## 21. Photos and media

Photos are shared only in the school group and never with names attached.

## 22. Heat and rain

On very hot or wet days the coach shortens the session and avoids the 600m loop. A shortened session still counts as a session.

## 23. Hydration

Every child carries water. Coaches pause for water every 15 minutes.

## 24. Girls' participation

Coaches make sure girls get equal turns in relays and drills.

## 25. Children who need attention

The coordinator decides with the coach what extra support a child receives: extra encouragement, adjusted drills, or a talk with the PE teacher.

## 26. No ranking

Children are never ranked or scored against each other. The aim is each child's own progress.

## 27. Coach handover

When a coach changes, the outgoing coach writes a short note on each child who needs attention.

## 28. Midline review

After the midline assessment the coordinator reviews every child with the coach and PE teacher.

## 29. End of cycle

At the end of six weeks each school gets a short summary of attendance and fitness change.

## 30. Changes to this guideline

Thresholds may change after review with HRS. The current values are synthetic placeholders.
