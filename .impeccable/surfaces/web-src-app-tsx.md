## Direction contract

THESIS: A coach's clipboard turned into a calm, bright dashboard: pastel stat tiles and a living calendar on top, and every number a tappable chip that opens the spreadsheet cell it came from. Refuses the grey admin table and the four-bin traffic light.

OWN-WORLD: Pale blue-lavender ground; white 28px cards; pastel tiles (lavender, sky, mint, peach) each with a black rounded icon tile, big tabular number and a black pill; large 3D renders (Fluent 3D, MIT) half-faded in tile corners; one orange accent for the line chart, today and primary actions; green for improvement. Floating pill nav of circular icon buttons, black active circle. System SF stack, tabular numerals. Dark promo card for the next meet.

STORY: The manager sees how the programme is moving, who stands out, what meet is next and how many sheets need checking; taps through to a child and sees the exact cell.

Phone: greeting, horizontally scrolling KPI tiles, next-meet card, lists; floating bottom pill tab bar.
